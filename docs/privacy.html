Privacy Policy for Life OS

Last updated: July 2026

Life OS is a personal desktop productivity tool. All data generated or stored by Life OS — tasks, projects, documents, notes, and settings — is kept entirely on your own local machine.

We do not collect, transmit, share, or store any personal data or analytics on external servers. Life OS has no account system, no telemetry, and no network calls to any server operated by the developer. The application works fully offline.

If you have any questions about this policy, contact the developer directly at github.com/J0hnWIcks.
